Welcome

Political scientist studying how the far right becomes normal – and what mainstream actors do about it.

I am a Research Fellow at the Chair of Political Sociology & Social Politics at Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin. In 2025, I completed my Ph.D. in Political Science there (summa cum laude) with a dissertation on the role of mainstream actors in the normalisation of far-right politics, supervised by Hanna Schwander, with Gökce Yurdakul and Leonie de Jonge on the committee.

My research asks how mainstream parties respond to far-right challengers, and how those responses – together with media representation and public attitudes – shape the boundaries of acceptable politics in liberal democracies. I am also involved in work on political polarisation and support for militant democracy, and I have been a visiting researcher at King’s College London, Uppsala University and the University of Bath.

On this website you can find my research, my teaching, and my CV. Feel free to reach out if you would like to chat
